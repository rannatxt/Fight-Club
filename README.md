# Neon Legends — World Tour

A self-contained anime-inspired arcade fighter built with Canvas 2D and procedural Web Audio. No libraries, downloads, build steps, or external art assets are required.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern desktop browser and choose **Start Fight**. The game requests fullscreen when the match begins; the in-game Full Screen button can toggle it later. On phones, turn the device sideways for the wide arena and use the touch controls.

## Controls

- **A / D:** move; **W:** jump; **S:** duck; **I:** guard
- **O:** punch; **P:** kick
- The two large round attack buttons sit on the right on touch screens. Movement, jump, duck, and guard controls sit on the left.
- **Esc:** pause or resume

## Fighter and arenas

Name your fighter, choose a balanced, aggressive, or technical personality, and select a crimson, midnight, or ivory outfit. The chosen look is saved in the browser.

- **China:** Dragon Tower, a moonlit pagoda rooftop
- **India:** Vidhana Soudha-inspired Bengaluru stage
- **USA:** Main Event Dome, a championship arena
- **Dubai:** Burj Afterdark, a night skyline and desert rooftop
- **Japan:** Neon Crossing in Tokyo
- **United Kingdom:** Clocktower Bridge in London

## Survival circuit and sound

Defeat the CPU to survive each round. The rival grows tougher as the run continues. Score, rounds survived, and your personal best appear on the end card and are saved in this browser. The original moody martial-arts synth music and impact effects are generated by Web Audio; no song upload is needed.
