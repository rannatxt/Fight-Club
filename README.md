# Dune Warriors — World Tour

A standalone shadow-style fighting game built with Canvas 2D and Web Audio. No build tools, libraries, or external art assets are required.

## Start

Open `index.html` in a modern browser and press **Start Fight**. The game expands to fullscreen when the match starts. Choose an arena, fighter name, personality, outfit, and weapon first.

## Controls

- **A / D:** move; **W:** jump; **S:** crouch; **I:** guard
- **O:** punch; **P:** kick; **Q:** weapon strike
- On phones, turn sideways for landscape play. Use the round movement pad on the left; punch, kick, and weapon buttons are stacked on the right.
- **Esc:** pause or resume

## Bosses, coins, and weapons

Survive the next round to earn coins. A boss arrives every third round and pays a larger reward. Your wallet, best score, and best round count save in the browser.

- **Katana:** included
- **Naginata:** unlock for 120 coins; extra reach and damage
- **Twin blades:** unlock for 250 coins; quicker strikes

## Music

Press **Load MP3** and choose the `Ninja in the Night` file from your device. It plays during each round and pauses at the round result. The game restarts it for the next round. The selected file stays on your device and must be selected again in a new browser session; it is not bundled in the public repository. Without a loaded file, the game uses its built-in synthesized music and impact sounds.

## Arenas

China’s Dragon Tower, India’s Vidhana Soudha, the USA Main Event Dome, Dubai’s Burj Afterdark, Tokyo’s Neon Crossing, and London’s Clocktower Bridge.
